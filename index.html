Guozhen (Gordon) Ji

I am an empirical IO economist and Associate at Econic Partners focusing on antitrust litigation and merger reviews.

My research examines the intersection of structural economics and public policy, with particular attention to optimal pricing in environmental and transportation markets. I apply structural industrial organization methods, causal inference, and machine learning to large-scale data in the study of market power, pricing, and market dynamics.

I received my Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Texas at Austin in May 2026, specializing in Empirical Industrial Organization, and Econometrics.

Outside of research, I enjoy live electronic music, strategy games, and exploring the local culinary scene while traveling.
